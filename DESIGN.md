## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an ultra-luxurious, cinematic, spatial computing interface inspired by high-end spatial audio-visual hardware (Apple Vision Pro) and cutting-edge digital physics (Lusion.co). It redefines professional clinical environments, surgical planning tools, and luxury medical-grade health tech into an aspirational, dark-universe spatial realm.

The aesthetic fuses **Spatial Glassmorphism** with **Futuristic Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, infinite obsidian voids layered with suspended refractive optics.
- **Physicality:** Dynamic specular light reflections, cold liquid metallic chrome accents, and soft diffuse bioluminescent halos.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Uncompromising clinical authority, serene precision, calm intelligence, and bespoke luxury.
- **Target Audience:** Visionary healthcare specialists, advanced diagnostic operators, and high-net-worth patients seeking the pinnacle of technological sophistication and modern surgical elegance.

## Colors
The palette balances cold, impenetrable void depths with hyper-precise refractive light emissions.

- **Obsidian Dark Void Canvas (`#030712`, `#090d16`):** Absorbs surrounding ambient clutter, acting as the deep cinematic base plate for floating volumetric cards and spatial glass surfaces.
- **Icy Platinum & Spectral Cyan (`#38bdf8` / `#818cf8`):** Serves as optical guidance beacons, focal focus rings, live instrument signals, and refined bioluminescent telemetry.
- **Liquid Metallic Silver (`#e2e8f0`, `#cbd5e1`, `#94a3b8`):** Emulates polished surgical chrome, brushed titanium hardware fixtures, and high-tension metallic boundaries.
- **Refractive Alpha Layers:** Glass surfaces leverage pure whites at 2% to 6% opacity layered over deep backdrop blurs, ensuring maximum clarity without visual turbidity.

## Typography
Typography is architectural, crisp, and high-fidelity:
- **Display & Section Titles (`Syne`):** Imparts an avant-garde, sculpted edge reminiscent of bespoke high-fashion and speculative architectural monographs.
- **Editorial & Body Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine legibility, humane geometry, and balanced rhythm for clinical data, operator instructions, and procedural logs.
- **Micro-Metrics & HUD Telemetry (`Inter`):** Renders patient indicators, sensor readings, and pill status tags with strict typographic discipline and tracked-out optical neutrality.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates as a spatial viewport using an intentional 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for widescreen diagnostics, spatial tablets, and compact field handhelds:

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `3.5rem` safe outer margins, preserving breathing room around floating refractive consoles.
- **Tablet / Spatial Viewport (768px - 1439px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Side panels dock as translucent lateral sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Stacks complex data visualizers into vertically scrolling frosted cards.
- **Rhythm:** Modular increments based on a strict 4px/8px micro-grid ensure pixel-aligned glass bounding boxes and consistent specular border highlights.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized entirely through optical refraction and light dispersion rather than traditional opaque drop shadows:

1. **Backdrop Diffusion:** Surfaces employ multi-pass frosted blurs ranging from `backdrop-blur(24px)` for elevated panels to `backdrop-blur(36px)` for modal dialogs and spatial overlays.
2. **Refractive Specular Trim:** Glass sheets feature an ultra-thin perimeter stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) with directional top-to-bottom gradients (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.01)`), mimicking light catching the bevel of precision-cut sapphire crystal.
3. **Ambient Bioluminescent Cast:** Floating actionable panels project a deep, diffuse radiant halo: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 40px -8px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)`.
4. **Specular Sheen Passes:** Surfaces incorporate a faint diagonal linear sheen sweep from top-left to bottom-right, accentuating curvature and physical mass.

## Shapes
Geometry is sculpted with refined micro-radii that invoke optical precision lens caps and luxury hardware:

- **Primary Cards & Floating Canvas (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Balanced curvature that softens technological austerity while retaining disciplined architectural structure.
- **Action Elements & Buttons (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Tactile, confident geometric anchors.
- **Telemetry Chips & Status Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Autonomous capsule-shaped pods floating over the refractive substrate, communicating operational readiness.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary Liquid Metallic CTA:** Built with a reflective silver-to-platinum gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #f8fafc 0%, #cbd5e1 50%, #94a3b8 100%)`), dark obsidian label typography (`#030712`), and an inner directional highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`). Hover triggers an icy blue outer flare (`0 0 24px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Ultra-translucent core (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and text rendered in metallic silver (`#e2e8f0`).

### Luxury Telemetry Pills & Chips
- **Status Pods:** Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`) featuring a micro pulsating indicator beacon (3px cyan or platinum dot with soft ping keyframe animation).
- **Surface:** Backed with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` and a `1px` rim of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Spatial Diagnostic Panels
- **Backdrop:** Composed of layered frosted glass (`backdrop-blur(28px)`, background `rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.65)`).
- **Beveling:** Top edge features a subtle `1px` white specular highlight line, falling off into subtle obsidian translucency at the bottom edge.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Surface:** Dark recessed inset (`background: rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Focus State:** Transitions border to solid icy cyan (`#38bdf8`) with an inner luminous shadow `inset 0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Toggles:** Sculpted pill-track (`#090d16`) with an extruded liquid chrome thumb that glides into place, glowing with an electric indigo/cyan aura when engaged.
- **Checkboxes:** Squared with `4px` corner radius, metallic stroke, and a glowing cyan checkmark vector on activation.

### Spatial Patient & Procedure Cards
- **Structure:** Framed with chrome-tinted avatar viewports, clinical telemetry readouts in `Inter` label caps, and an integrated real-time schedule indicator ring.
